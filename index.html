Luka Džarić

Luka Džarić is a Montenegrin designer based in Slovenia. Džarić is pronounced JAR-ICH. He has worked as a brand, motion, type, UI/UX and editorial designer on commercial projects for brands and clients, as well as on academic projects and exhibitions. He is currently finishing an MFA at the Academy of Fine Arts and Design in Ljubljana.
